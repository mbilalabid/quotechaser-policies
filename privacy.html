1. Information we process

  • Account information: your name, email address, account identifier, authentication information, business name and preferences.
  • Quotes and follow-ups: client details, services, amounts, currencies, dates, notes, quote status, follow-up schedules and completion records that you enter.
  • AI features: generated drafts and usage records needed to provide the feature and enforce plan limits.
  • Subscriptions: account identifiers, products, purchase and entitlement status, and relevant transaction information processed through RevenueCat and the applicable app store. The app does not collect full payment-card details.
  • Technical information: our service providers may process IP addresses, request timestamps, device or app information and diagnostic records to operate and secure their services.
  • Support correspondence: information you provide when contacting us.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, store and display quotes, organize follow-ups, generate requested drafts, verify subscriptions, enforce usage limits, respond to support requests and protect the service against misuse.

3. AI-generated drafts

When you consent to and request AI generation, our backend sends relevant information to OpenAI. This includes the client name, service, quote amount and currency, time since the quote was sent, follow-up number, selected tone, quote notes and business name.

Only enter client information you are authorized to use. Avoid including sensitive information that is unnecessary for the draft.

Our AI requests disable response storage using the store: false setting. This does not disable all provider security or operational retention. OpenAI's handling remains subject to its applicable service terms and data controls.

Generated drafts are saved in QuoteChaser. You can use quote tracking without requesting AI generation. QuoteChaser does not automatically send follow-up messages.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use the following services:

  • Supabase for account authentication and database storage.
  • OpenAI for AI generation requested by you.
  • RevenueCat for subscription management.
  • The applicable app store for purchase processing.

Our current primary Supabase database is hosted in Singapore. Other providers may process information in other countries.

If you choose to share a draft through email, WhatsApp or another application, that service receives the content you share and handles it under its own terms and privacy policy.

We may disclose information where necessary to comply with applicable law, protect users or address misuse. The current app does not include advertising services or a feature for selling user data.

5. Device storage and notifications

The app stores session and onboarding information locally. Native authentication sessions use operating-system secure storage.

With your permission, reminders are scheduled on your device. Notification content may appear on your lock screen depending on your settings. You can disable notifications through your device settings.

6. Retention and deletion requests

Account and quote information is retained to provide the service while your account is active. You can delete individual quotes. Successful AI usage records may remain for quota accounting after a quote is deleted, while the generated message content is redacted.

For access, correction or account-deletion requests, contact mbilalabid46789@gmail.com using your registered email address. For deletion requests, use the subject “QuoteChaser account deletion”. Do not send your password or API keys.

We may need to verify account ownership. The deletion workflow, completion timeframe and retention periods for support correspondence, security logs, subscription records and provider backups are still being finalized. This draft does not promise a fixed deletion deadline.

Deleting an account does not cancel a store subscription. Manage or cancel your subscription through the store where it was purchased.

7. Your choices and security

You can update supported profile and quote fields, decline AI generation and control notification permissions. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights regarding your personal information. Contact us to make a request.

We use access controls and encrypted transport to protect information. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your login credentials private.

8. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change and provide appropriate notice of material changes. An effective date will be added when the finalized policy takes effect.
